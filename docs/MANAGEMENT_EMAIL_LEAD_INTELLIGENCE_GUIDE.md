### Key Capabilities & New Features Covered in the Guide

1. **Streamlined 1-Click Master Enrichment (`Run Complete Intel`)**
   * Reps can now run both the deep **AI Website Crawler** and live **Agent Reach Web & LinkedIn indexing** simultaneously with a single click.
   * Eliminates the need for multiple manual scans; the entire 360-degree company profile loads in 5–8 seconds.

2. **LinkedIn Operational Decision Maker Discovery & 1-Click CRM Sync**
   * The engine specifically filters and indexes operational personnel who hold shipping and courier budget authority (*Logistics Managers, Warehouse Heads, Operations Directors, Managing Directors*).
   * **`Primary Decision Maker`** / **`High Match`** badges guide reps straight to the right person, bypassing gatekeepers.
   * Includes direct **`[LinkedIn ↗]`** verification links and a high-contrast **`[+ Add to Contacts]`** button that permanently saves the executive into the lead’s CRM contact directory with zero manual data entry.

3. **Live Logistics & Incumbent Courier Signals**
   * Automatically detects existing parcel carriers (*Australia Post, StarTrack, Aramex, Sendle*), daily dispatch cutoff times, warehouse fulfillment locations, and multi-store retail footprints.
   * Replaces raw walls of website text with a **4-bullet AI Shipping Policy Summary** scannable in 5 seconds.

4. **Social Footprint & Competitor Delivery Complaint Tracking**
   * Detects active Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn channels to size daily outbound parcel volume (e.g., 50k+ followers = high daily satchel volume).
   * Scans public delivery feedback to identify customer complaints regarding delayed parcels or lost boxes with competitor couriers—giving reps a warm, pain-point-driven conversation hook.

5. **Actionable Pre-Call Assets (Suggested Opener & Personalisation)**
   * Every enriched lead provides a tailored, word-for-word cold call opener referencing their actual warehouse locations, store footprint, and delivery habits, allowing reps to transition into confident, high-converting conversations immediately.
